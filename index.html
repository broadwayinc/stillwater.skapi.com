Stillwater Lake

Come as you are. Cast a line, share a kind word, and let the quiet water give your spirit room to rest. We’re glad you’re here.

You were away for a while, so your spot at the lake was let go. Walk back in to rejoin everyone.

Walk down to the water, cast a line, gather branches and stones for a campfire, and grill what you catch while it burns. Anyone at the lake can sit down and eat from your grill, and every fish eaten earns an appreciation dance to share whenever you like.

You are inside the app's built-in browser. The lake works better in your normal browser.
